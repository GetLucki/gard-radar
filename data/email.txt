   Survival: 18.6 ha, of which 15 ha is forest with good growth, so firewood is covered for good, and about 2 ha of arable plus 1.5 ha of pasture covers a garden and a few animals. The listing mentions no well, no wood heating and no surface water within 500 m, and the buildings are described as in poorer condition, so the house is unlikely to cope with standing empty without work first. It sits well off the main roads between Tidaholm, Hjo and Habo, 2.1 h from Göteborg.
   Investment: The broker puts the standing timber at 2 595 m3sk, which at the southern price of about 930 kr per m3sk is worth roughly 2 400 000 kr, the same as the whole asking price. At 129 310 kr per ha it is well under the 227 861 kr median, so in effect we would get the land for the price of the forest and the buildings thrown in for free. Västra Götaland inland is steady but not fast to resell, and a forest holding sells mostly to neighbours and forest buyers, not to families.
   Why this rank: Best value per hectare among the newcomers and among the best in the 2 to 3.5 MSEK band, held back because the buildings fail the low-maintenance spec and probably need either a total renovation or a new house.
   Maintenance: Buildings described as in poorer condition, build year, facade, roof and windows not stated; assume a full renovation or a rebuild within ten years.
   Recommendation: Ask the broker for X first: the condition of the dwelling house, whether it is habitable, the water source and the avlopp status, plus the skogsbruksplan, since this is a strong land buy only if the house problem has a price we can accept.

   Scores: overall 17, survival 7, invest 27
   Survival: Only 4.4 ha with no forest mentioned, so no firewood of our own and little room for both pasture and garden. No well, no lake or stream and no wood heating are stated, and the listing is still at the coming-soon stage with almost no description. At 2.4 h from Göteborg it sits near the top of our drive-time limit.
   Investment: At 1 079 545 kr per ha it costs almost five times the 227 861 kr median, so nearly all the money goes into the house rather than the land. A 152 m2 house from 1909 is most likely painted timber, which is exactly what the low-maintenance spec rules out. Kronoberg has a strong fritidshus market, but that does not make up for the price.
   Why this rank: Near the bottom of the 3.5 to 5 MSEK band: at this price other listings offer 10 to 30 ha with forest.
   Maintenance: Built 1909, facade, roof and windows not stated; expect painting, window and possibly roof work within ten years unless a recent total renovation is documented.
   Recommendation: Skip, because it is too little land at far too high a price per hectare, with an old house that probably needs regular upkeep.
