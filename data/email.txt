49. 23,5 ha på Vaberget, Karlsborg, Karlsborg · Skaraborg/Vättern · 23.5 ha · 2.4 h, 3 000 000 kr
   Scores: overall 44, survival 23, invest 65
   Survival: About 20.3 ha of productive spruce forest gives firewood indefinitely, and there is shared road access plus a ladugård, a shed and a storage building. There is no dwelling at all, no well is mentioned and no surface water is stated (the listing puts water more than 500 m away). As a safe house it fails the basic purpose until a house, well and avlopp are built, which means bygglov and several years of work.
   Investment: The standing volume of 3 533 m3sk at the southern price of roughly 930 kr per m3sk is worth about 3.3 MSEK, above the 3 000 000 kr minimum, and 127 660 kr per ha is far below the 227 723 kr median. It is sold by public auction on 2026-11-06 ordered by the tingsrätt, so the final price will likely land above the minimum, and about 2 181 m3sk can be felled within the plan period, which gives real thinning and harvest income.
   Why this rank: A strong forest investment, but it has no house and no water, which the plan treats as must-haves, so it sits well below every listing in the 2.0 to 3.5 MSEK band that has a finished house.
   Maintenance: No house to maintain; the barn, shed and storage building have unknown roofs and condition, and the forest needs a skogsbruksplan and planned felling rather than building upkeep.
   Recommendation: Skip, because there is no dwelling or water and building a safe house from scratch goes against the turnkey rule; revisit only if you ever want a separate forest investment.
