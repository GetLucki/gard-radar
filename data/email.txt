135 listings match the filters today. Nothing new came in, no prices moved, and one dropped off: Hundsjövägen 5 in Alingsås at 3 795 000 kr. The median ask is still 2 995 000 kr, or 227 723 kr per ha. Småland (44) and Dalsland/Värmland (31) have most of the supply, and Göteborg hinterland is the dearest at a 3 550 000 kr median. The five listings judged today were already on the market and fill empty places on the board. 10. Abrahamsryd 5, Älmhult · Småland · 4.0 ha · 170 m² · 2.3 h, 1 695 000 kr
   Scores: overall 58, survival 68, invest 48
   Survival: Dug well, wood boiler with two accumulator tanks and a lake nearby make it workable off-grid for water and heat. But 4 ha of infield gives pasture and garden, not firewood. The 410 m2 barn is in poor condition.
   Investment: As listed, 4 ha at 1 695 000 kr is 423 750 kr per ha, far above the median. The full farm of 41 ha for 4 295 000 kr comes out near 105 000 kr per ha with the house included, and that is the real investment case if much of it is forest. A year on Booli means the seller is waiting.
   Why this rank: The 4 ha split is weak and the old timber house fails the spec. The 41 ha option puts it in the same league as Övergården, which is why it is not a plain skip.
   Maintenance: Old timber house with a new roof and bathroom, but walls left unfinished, older avlopp and a run-down barn. Expect painting, interior work and a new sewage system within ten years.
   Recommendation: Ask the broker for X first: the forest share and timber volume on the full 41 ha option at 4 295 000 kr. The 4 ha split on its own is a Skip.

13. Gunnarsjö Bro 1, Varberg · Halland inland · 15.5 ha · 123 m² · byggår 1964 · 1.0 h, 3 395 000 kr
   Scores: overall 58, survival 59, invest 57
   Survival: 15.5 ha with forest and open land near water, two working vedspisar, solar cells and a barn with workshop and slaughter room. That covers heat and food well. Water source is not stated, and an 1850s timber cottage next door adds space but needs care. At 1 h from Göteborg it is the easiest to reach in today's batch.
   Investment: At 219 032 kr per ha it is just under the median, in Halland inland, which tops every price series and resells easily. The house is from 1964, just short of the 1965 line. It has bergvärme and solar, so running costs are low. Four months on Booli suggests there is room to negotiate.
   Why this rank: Best location and liquidity of the five, with fair land pricing. Held back by an unknown facade and water source, an older bathroom and a lot of outbuildings to keep up.
   Maintenance: Built 1964, facade and roof material not stated, bathroom older standard. The 1850s timber house and the ladugård will need paint and roof work within ten years.
   Recommendation: Ask the broker for X first: the water source (drilled or dug well), facade and roof material, and how many of the 15.5 ha are productive forest. If it is a drilled well and the house is rendered or brick, book a viewing.

39. Övergården, Säffle · Dalsland/Värmland · 29.0 ha · 2.2 h, 3 750 000 kr
   Scores: overall 49, survival 33, invest 65
   Survival: About 23 ha of productive forest gives firewood for good, and 5 ha of arable land covers food. The listing says nothing about well, heating or how the farmstead sits, so water and heat are unknowns. At 2.2 h from Göteborg it is inside the limit, but only just.
   Investment: The broker puts the standing timber at about 5 300 m3sk, around 236 per ha. Even at the middle-Sweden price of about 561 kr per m3sk that is roughly 3 000 000 kr of forest, plus arable land, against 3 750 000 kr asked. At 129 310 kr per ha it is well below the 227 723 kr median, so the house and yard come almost free. Areal is a forest specialist and Värmland villas are up 9.1 percent, but parts of Säffle may need a förvärvstillstånd.
   Why this rank: The strongest land value per krona in the 3.5 to 5 MSEK band. The house is the brake: the broker calls it in need of renovation, which is exactly what the spec rules out.
   Maintenance: Build year, facade and roof unknown, and the house is stated as needing renovation, so expect a full overhaul or a replacement house within ten years.
   Recommendation: Ask the broker for X first: the skogsbruksplan, the house's build year, facade, roof, heating and water source, and whether a förvärvstillstånd is needed. The forest maths only works if we can live with or replace the house.
   https://www.hemnet.se/bostad/gard-saffle-kommun-overgarden-21891709

   Recommendation: Skip, an 18th century house and four outbuildings at a high price per ha fail the low-maintenance bar.
