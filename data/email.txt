   Survival: About 9 ha of fairly stone-free arable and pasture right by the yard, which is excellent for food and animals, but under 0.5 ha of forest, so firewood would have to be bought or planted. No well and no wood heating are mentioned, and a gårdshotell with a restaurant a short walk away means the place is neither secluded nor quiet. At 1.3 h from Göteborg it is easy to reach and the bygd is clearly alive.
   Investment: At 188 224 kr per ha it sits below the 227 723 kr median, and 9 ha of good Halland arable land is worth roughly 1 to 2 MSEK alone, so the house comes cheap. The value is in arable land, not forest, which is steadier but grows more slowly than southern forest. Halland inland resells well and the low price keeps the buyer pool wide.
   Why this rank: The cheapest solid farm in its band with real food land close to Göteborg, held back by almost no forest and no facts on the house.
   Maintenance: Unknown: build year, facade, roof and windows are not stated; a 1.5-plan house with basement, ladugård, brygghus and garage means several outbuildings to maintain.
   Recommendation: Ask the broker for build year, facade and roof material, window age, heating system and well details first, because the land and price are right but the house is a blank.
