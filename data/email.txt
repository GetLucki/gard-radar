Four months on Booli means the seller should take an offer. Weakness for resale is the outbuilding stock: an 1850s timmerhus, hönshus and smedja look charming but add nothing to what a buyer like us will pay.
   Why this rank: Closest, most land and most installed systems of the five; the 1964 build year sits one year under the 1965 line and is treated as the same era. It only stays at the top if the facade and roof turn out to be brick, render or metal.
   Maintenance: Built 1964, 1.5 floors with basement; facade, roof and windows not stated. Within ten years: the older upstairs bathroom, probably window replacement, and a decision to demolish or roof the 1850s timmerhus, hönshus and smedja, which are the real maintenance load here. Empty-house risks: basement damp if the drainage is old, and five separate buildings to secure against break-ins.
   Recommendation: Ask the broker for facade, roof and window materials and the water source first, then book a viewing; the main house is the right era and the land and systems are already in place.
